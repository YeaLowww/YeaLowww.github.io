Assassin's Creed Inspired RPG (UE5)
Pet project inspired by the Assassin's Creed series, built in Unreal Engine 5 using Blueprints. Implemented stealth, parkour movement, melee combat, RPG progression, and branching choices affecting gameplay flow. Focused on building modular gameplay systems and experimenting with level-driven mechanics. Great exercise in game design thinking and rapid prototyping in UE5.
